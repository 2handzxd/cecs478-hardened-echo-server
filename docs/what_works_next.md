\## Initial Results



\- Packet capture (PCAP) shows that message contents are not directly readable, indicating secure transmission behavior.

\- Valid messages are successfully processed and logged by the server.

\- Invalid or malformed inputs are rejected and recorded in logs.

\- Rate limiting triggers when repeated requests exceed the defined threshold.

\- Exported metrics (CSV/JSON) summarize system behavior, including accepted and rejected messages.



These results demonstrate that the system correctly enforces validation, logging, and rate limiting while providing observable evidence through artifacts.
